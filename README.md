# ShopSteward Agreement & Onboarding Certificate

A pixel-perfect, print-ready HTML/CSS template matching the official ShopSteward Service Agreement and Onboarding Certificate.

## Features
- **A4 Strict Compatibility**: Designed to standard A4 specifications (`210mm x 297mm`) with `@page` and `break-after: page` print rules.
  - Page 1: Header, Legal Parties (01) to Hardware Requirements (08).
  - Page 2: Data Ownership (09) to Service Uptime (16).
  - Page 3: Data Export (17) to Jurisdiction (23) + Signatures Container.
  - Page 4: Certificate of Onboarding with decorative borders, personalized welcome letter, login credentials card, and validation seals.
- **Dedicated Client Info Block**: Change Shop Name, Owner Name, Phone, Address, and Login Credentials live without touching legal code.
- **Dedicated Subscription & Tier Block**: Easily adjust Tier Name, Setup Fee, and Annual Subscription Amount.
- **Signature & Seal Slots**:
  - Upload your **Founder Signature PNG** and **Official Seal PNG** directly from the top toolbar, OR drop `signature.png` and `seal.png` into this folder.
  - Changes persist across reloads via browser `localStorage`.
- **Watermark & Anti-Counterfeit**: Angled, subtle `ShopSteward` watermark printed across the agreement pages.

## How to Use & Print
1. Open [`index.html`](./index.html) in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave).
2. Click **"Edit Details"** in the top bar to update client info, pricing, or upload your PNG images.
3. Click **"Print / Save as PDF (A4)"** (or press `Ctrl + P`).
4. In the print dialog:
   - **Destination**: *Save as PDF* or your physical printer.
   - **Paper size**: *A4*
   - **Margins**: *None* or *Default*
   - **Options**: Enable **"Background graphics"** (to print the watermarks and subtle table tints).
